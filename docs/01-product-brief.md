# Product Brief
**Status:** Approved V1 baseline (2026-10-14); delivery pending.

 Commitment Counter is a free-to-use anonymous pledge projection utility for churches and nonprofits.

It records one-time, weekly, monthly, and per-service commitments and projects totals for an inclusive campaign period. V1 includes a same-browser live second-screen display, Excel XLSX reporting, and local JSON backup/restore. It excludes donor identity, payment processing, accounting, cloud sync, and cross-device collaboration. The operator should be able to set up a campaign in minutes and enter a pledge in seconds.

All decisions are proposed, subject to owner approval.
